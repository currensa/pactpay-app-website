Overview

What is PactPay

PactPay is a non-custodial crypto payment platform. Organisations can define custom payment rules and schedules, then execute payments to their members through smart contracts. No fund custody. No KYC required.

PactPay is built for Web3 teams, DAOs, and any organisation that pays contributors in crypto currency and values privacy, on-chain transparency, and self-custody.

Core principles

  1. Self-Custody by Default Funds stay in the payer's control at all times. No third party is involved. Payment logic is defined and signed by the payer. The smart contract can only execute the transaction and has no authority over the funds themselves.
  2. Anonymous by Design A single EOA address is all you need to register, pay, and receive. No identity binding is required.
  3. Invisible Identity Layer Your identity is used for verification but never exposed. Only a shielded identity exists on-chain. Your real address is untraceable.
  4. Dual-Address Isolation Your shielded identity and your receiving address are fully isolated from each other. Both can be changed independently at any time. Every payment flow has built-in protection.

Getting Started

How it works

PactPay has two roles: Organisation (the paying party) and Member (the receiving party). The entire flow happens on-chain with no intermediary.

Note: Payments are not sent automatically. Members must actively claim their payment after the claim time arrives.

Organisation flow

  1. Connect your wallet or sign in with email and register
  2. Create your organisation
  3. Invite members
  4. Create and execute payment transactions

Member flow

  1. Register an account, connect your EOA wallet, and complete two signing steps to generate your shielded identity
  2. Join an organisation
  3. Wait for the organisation to create and execute a transaction
  4. Once the claim time arrives, claim your payment to any wallet address via your shielded identity

For organisations

  1. Go to pactpay.app and connect your wallet or sign in with email.
  2. Create your organisation and fill in the basic details.
  3. Go to Members, click Invite Members, share the invite link, and approve incoming members.
  4. Configure payment settings, including chain and token type.
  5. On the Members page, set the payment amount for each member.
  6. Go to Transactions, click New Transaction, select members, confirm amounts, and set a claim time.
  7. Review the transaction summary and click Sign & Execute. Make sure to execute before the claim time.
  8. You can also assign other members as Admin or Operator to help manage and execute transactions. See the permission table below for details.

For members

  1. Go to pactpay.app, connect your EOA wallet (used for login and claim signing), and complete registration.
  2. Use the invite link or code from your organisation to join, then wait for approval.
  3. Once the organisation executes a payment transaction, you will receive an email notification. You can then check the claim time.
  4. When the claim time arrives, go to the platform and claim your payment.

Mechanism

Why funds are truly non-custodial

Many platforms claim to be secure, but funds actually pass through platform-controlled accounts. PactPay works differently:

Your EOA wallet
    ↓  Sign and initiate transaction
Payment smart contract (on-chain, publicly verifiable)
    ↓  Unlocks automatically when claim time arrives
Member's receiving wallet

There is no PactPay-controlled account anywhere in this path. The payment smart contract logic is fully public. Anyone can verify on-chain exactly what it does and does not do. PactPay as a platform holds no keys and has no authority to move funds inside the contract.

Contract addresses:

Sepolia Testnet: 0x5F2E184B850E552f1B66f754EAD5d278475f6811

When an operator creates and signs a transaction, the following parameters are encoded together into the on-chain transaction:

  • The recipient list (each member's shielded identity address)
  • The amount for each recipient
  • The claim time

Once signed, these parameters are locked. Neither PactPay nor the operator can modify them without creating a new transaction. This is what "payment logic defined and locked by the payer's signature" means in practice.

Specifically:

The platform cannot alter it unilaterally. Even if PactPay's servers go offline, the claim time encoded in the contract remains valid and enforceable.

Two execution scenarios behave differently:

  • Execute before claim time: Funds enter the contract. The contract checks block.timestamp < claimTime and rejects claim requests until the time condition is met.
  • Execute after claim time: When funds enter the contract, block.timestamp >= claimTime is already satisfied. Members can claim immediately.

Members pull funds actively — nothing is pushed. This guarantees that funds can only leave the contract when the member themselves signs the claim transaction. PactPay cannot withdraw on a member's behalf.

How your address is protected

PactPay's identity layer is built on a shielded identity mechanism:

  • When you register, the system derives an encrypted shielded identity from your EOA address and stores it on-chain.
  • All on-chain transaction records reference only the shielded identity address. Your real EOA is never directly linked.
  • Your shielded identity and your receiving address are independent of each other. Both can be changed at any time.

This achieves two things:

  • Verification: The contract can confirm that an operation is authorised.
  • Privacy: No one can infer from on-chain records who performed the operation.

Reference

Pricing

No monthly subscription. You pay per execution.

Fee calculation example: Executing a payment to 5 members at the early access rate costs 5 × 10 USDC = 50 USDC in service fees, plus gas (paid by the organisation's wallet directly to the network, separate from PactPay). The service fee is charged when the first member claims. If no one claims, no fee is incurred. Gas fees for member claims are covered by PactPay.

For large teams or high-frequency payment needs, contact support@pactpay.app to discuss a custom plan.

FAQ

For organisations

When is the service fee charged? The service fee is charged when the first member claims after a payment is executed. If no members claim, no fee is incurred.

What happens if I execute a transaction after the claim time has passed? Funds are released immediately upon entering the contract. Members can claim right away without waiting.

Can I modify member payment amounts after a transaction is executed? No. Amounts are locked at the point of signing. To make adjustments, you will need to create a new transaction.

Who pays the gas fee for executing a transaction? The organisation's wallet pays the gas fee. This fee is charged directly by the blockchain network and is separate from PactPay.

Can I cancel a transaction after it has been executed? Yes. You can request a refund at any time before all members have completed their claims.

For members

How do I join an organisation? Ask your organisation for an invite link or invite code. After registering, your membership will be pending until the organisation approves it.

Why can't I see a Claim button? Either the claim time has not arrived yet, or the organisation has not executed the transaction. Check the transaction status in the Data Summary on your home page, or contact your organisation to confirm.

Where are my funds before I claim? Your funds are held in the payment smart contract, locked until the claim time. The contract content is publicly verifiable on-chain.

Can I change my login or claim wallet? Only if you have no unclaimed transactions. If unclaimed transactions exist, changing your wallet will break the signature match and those transactions will no longer be claimable.

Can I change my receiving wallet? Yes. You can update your receiving wallet address at any time during the claim process. The new address takes effect immediately for that claim.

Do I need to do anything before the claim time? Nothing at all. Wait for the claim time to arrive. You will receive an email notification when it is time to return to the platform and claim.

Do I need to pay gas to claim? No. Gas fees for member claims are covered by PactPay.
