Testing PactPay on Sepolia Testnet

This guide walks you through setting up a test environment and sending your first payroll transaction on the Sepolia testnet. No real funds are used.

Prerequisites

  • MetaMask (or any EOA wallet) installed in your browser
  • A PactPay organization‘s account (go to pactpay.app and register)

Step 1 — Get Sepolia ETH (for gas)

You need Sepolia ETH to pay gas fees when executing transactions.

  1. Go to https://cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia
  1. Sign in with a Google account
  1. Enter your wallet address and request test ETH
  1. Wait 1–2 minutes — the ETH will appear in your wallet
Google's faucet is currently the most reliable option. If it's unavailable, try https://sepoliafaucet.com or https://www.alchemy.com/faucets/ethereum-sepolia

Step 2 — Get test USDC

Get USDC for payroll. You'll need testnet USDC from Circle's official faucet.

  1. Go to https://faucet.circle.com
  1. Select Ethereum Sepolia as the network
  1. Enter your wallet address
  1. Click Send — testnet USDC will arrive within a minute

Step 3 — Add Sepolia network to your wallet

If Sepolia is not already in your wallet:

In MetaMask:

  1. Open MetaMask and click the network selector at the top
  1. Click Add network
  1. Select Sepolia from the list of popular test networks
  1. Click Add to confirm

If it doesn't appear automatically, add it manually with these settings:

Step 4 — Add USDC token to your wallet

If your wallet won't display testnet USDC automatically — you need to add it manually.

In MetaMask:

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the Assets tab and click Import tokens
  1. Select Custom token
  1. Paste the contract address: 0x1c7D4B196Cb0C7B01d743Fbc6116a902379C7238
  1. Token symbol and decimals will fill in automatically, confirm they show USDC
  1. Click Add custom token → Import tokens

You can verify the USDC contract on Etherscan:
https://sepolia.etherscan.io/token/0x1c7d4b196cb0c7b01d743fbc6116a902379c7238

Step 5 — Configure payment settings in PactPay

  1. Log in to PactPay and go to Settings
  1. Under Payment settings, set:
    • Network: Sepolia Testnet
    • Token: USDC
  1. Save your settings

Step 6 — Add a member

Before creating a transaction, you need at least one active member in your organisation.

  1. Go to Members → click + Invite Members
  1. Share the invite link or code with your test account (or a colleague)
  1. The invited user registers on PactPay, joins your organisation, and binds their claim wallet
  1. Back in Members, click ✅ to approve the pending member — their status changes to Active
  1. Set their USDC amount using the edit icon in the Amount column

Step 7 — Create and execute a transaction

  1. Go to Transactions → click + New Transaction
  1. Enter a transaction name (e.g. "Test payroll")
  1. Select your active member and confirm the USDC amount
  1. Set a claim time — the date and time from which the member can claim:
    • Set it a few minutes in the future to test the waiting flow
    • Or set it in the past to test immediate claiming
  1. Review the summary — check the payout amount, service fee, and claim time, click Sign & execute
  1. Sign the transaction and you’ll set

Once confirmed on-chain, the member can go to My Payments and claim their USDC when the claim time arrives.

Troubleshooting

Transaction failed / out of gas
Make sure you have enough Sepolia ETH. Go back to Step 1 and request more from the faucet.

USDC not showing in wallet
Double-check that you've imported the token contract address correctly (Step 4) and that your wallet is on the Sepolia network.

Member can't see the Claim button
Either the claim time hasn't arrived yet, or the transaction hasn't been executed. Check the transaction status in your Transactions list.

Faucet not working
Circle's USDC faucet occasionally has rate limits. Wait a few minutes and try again, or use a different wallet address.
